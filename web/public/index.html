Sub-Saharan Africa, after the ribbon is cut

One broken pump shouldn't bring back a five-mile walk.

About 1 in 4 handpumps in sub-Saharan Africa is out of order. The donor's money built it; nothing is left to repair it.1

WellKeep pays the mechanic only after the village confirms the pump works. Every payment is public, on NEAR.

WellKeep

Built on NEAR: a keyless deterministic account running a global contract by hash, NEP-171 records for water points and households, NEAR's on-chain random seed, near-kit, and a NEAR wallet for donors and mechanics.

Built for NEAR HACKATHON. A testnet prototype: test money, a fictional village, not tested with any real community. Reads come straight from the public testnet RPC; only the gas-paying relayer goes through this server.
