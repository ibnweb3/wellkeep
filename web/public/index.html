Sub-Saharan Africa, after the ribbon is cut

The well was donated. Who pays when the pump breaks?

About 1 in 4 handpumps in sub-Saharan Africa is out of order. The donor's money built it; nothing is left to repair it.1

WellKeep pays the mechanic only after the village confirms the pump works. Every payment is public.
